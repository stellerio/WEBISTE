01

SCALE

That cloud
is bigger
than it looks.

A single thunderstorm can rise many miles into the atmosphere. What appears like a small puff from the ground can be a vast three-dimensional structure stretching across the sky.

02

WATER

Made from
millions of
tiny drops.

Clouds form when moist air cools and water vapor condenses around microscopic particles. Those droplets and ice crystals scatter sunlight, creating the soft shapes we see.

03

LIGHT

White is only
the beginning
of the story.

The color and brightness of a cloud depend on its thickness, particle size and the angle of incoming light. At sunrise and sunset, the same cloud can become gold, pink, violet or deep blue.

KEEP LOOKING UP

The sky is
never still.

Clouds are part of a constantly changing atmospheric system. Their shapes are temporary signatures of wind, temperature, moisture and sunlight.
