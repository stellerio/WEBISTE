AN IMMERSIVE ATMOSPHERIC STUDY

Look closer.
It is alive.

Clouds are moving volumes of water, ice, light, pressure and energy—not flat shapes painted across a sky.

01SCALE

That tiny cloud is enormous.

A cloud can stretch for miles while appearing small from the ground. Soft edges hide depth, motion and layers that change constantly.

02WATER

Millions of tiny droplets.

Clouds form when moist air rises, cools and condenses around microscopic particles. Ice crystals and liquid droplets scatter sunlight.

03LIGHT

White is only the start of the story.

The same cloud can become gold, pink, blue or violet as sunlight changes. Thickness and particle size control its glow.

KEEP LOOKING UP

The sky is never still.

Wind, temperature, moisture and sunlight continuously redraw the atmosphere.
